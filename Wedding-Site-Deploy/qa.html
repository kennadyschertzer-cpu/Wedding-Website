Q&A

How should I dress?

We ask all of our guests to be in semi-formal attire. We hope everyone dresses their best, Noah is breaking in his dancing shoes as we speak.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins promptly at 5:00pm, be fashionably late and you'll miss it. Cocktail hour and the reception are to immediately follow the ceremony.

Where should I park?

Right at the venue, Old Monroe Distilling Co. has plenty of space, so no need to circle the block. Just remember to actually drive yourself; we haven't figured out teleportation yet.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue size and capacity, we kindly ask that guests only RSVP those listed on their invites. We wouldn't want the food or bar line getting too long.

I am traveling from out of state, how do I get there?

We encourage you to visit the "Travel" portion of our website for more information. Whether you prefer smooth sailing in the skies or a scenic train ride, we got you covered.

Are kids welcome?

If your little ones are listed on your invite, they're more than welcome to celebrate with us. If we didn't include them by name, we kindly ask you leave them home for the night. Think of it as a well-earned date night.
